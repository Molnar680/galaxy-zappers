Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Andrew Molnar

No data collected. Galaxy Zappers does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.
No accounts. No sign-up or login is required. Player names you add for the family scoreboard stay on your device.
No ads, purchases, or tracking. The game has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
No online features. The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
On-device storage only. Progress, scores, shop stars, badges, and settings are stored only on your device and are removed if you delete the app.
App Store rating prompt. The game may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles this, and the developer receives no personal information from it.
Children. Galaxy Zappers is made for families. Because no data is collected, no information is collected from children.
Changes. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
