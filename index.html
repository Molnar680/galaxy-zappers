About the Game

Galaxy Zappers is a bright, family-friendly alien space shooter for iPhone and iPad. Blast waves of wiggly aliens, rescue floating astronauts, grab power-ups, and beat a giant mothership boss at the end of every world: Sunny Meadow, Twilight Hills, Frosty Peaks and Cosmic Storm.

Dedicated to my daughters, Elizabeth, Isabel & Emma.

How to Play

  • Drag with one thumb to steer your ship.
  • Hold the 🔥 button to keep firing, or tap it for single shots. You can move it to the left or right side in ⚙️ Settings.
  • When an alien flashes red ❗ it's about to dive at you — dodge it or zap it mid-dive for bonus points.
  • Don't shoot the floating astronauts — fly over them to rescue them.
  • Earn 🌟 shop stars by clearing levels, then spend them in the Star Shop on upgrades, new ships and laser colors.

Game Modes

Classic · World Map — 4 worlds of hand-made levels with a boss in each world, plus Mega boss rematches.
Random Challenge — a surprise mix every time.
Chill — no hearts lost, just relax.
Time Attack — 60 seconds, a new random mix every run.

FAQ

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Progress, shop stars, badges and family scores are saved automatically on your device.
Can the whole family play?
Yes. Add players from the menu and each one gets their own scores on the family board.
It's too hard (or too easy).
Pick 🌱 Easy, ⚡ Normal or 🔥 Hard in ⚙️ Settings, or play Chill mode where no hearts are lost.
How do I reset progress?
Open ⚙️ Settings > Reset. You can clear family high scores, map progress, shop stars & ships, badges, or everything. Each asks "Are you sure?" first and can't be undone.
Can I turn off the music but keep sound effects?
Yes. Music, Sound effects and Vibration each have their own switch in Settings.
Does the game need internet? Are there ads or purchases?
No. Galaxy Zappers plays fully offline and has no ads, no in-app purchases and no online features.
Does my progress move to a new device?
Progress is stored only on the device, so it doesn't sync between devices.
